Why this project exists

The portfolio presents student research with clear methods, project context, and pathways for reviewers, collaborators, and learners. It is designed to make the work understandable before asking anyone to evaluate or contact its author.

What the repository documents

  • A Next.js portfolio with CMS-editable pages and reusable UI components.
  • Structured metadata, a sitemap, robots directives, and accessible social sharing.
  • Content and architecture that make research claims easier to trace to their source.
  • Performance-oriented delivery that keeps nonessential metrics off the initial render path.
